Privacy Policy

Audio iNtelligence · Last updated: August 12, 2026

Audio iNtelligence ("the app", "we") identifies music from video, audio files, microphone listening, and sung/hummed melodies. This policy explains what data is handled and how it is used.

What you send

  • Identify (file): a video or audio file you choose is uploaded to the Audio iNtelligence server to identify the music.
  • Listen: a short microphone recording is uploaded to identify music playing nearby.
  • Sing: a short recording of you singing, humming, or whistling is uploaded for melody-based recognition.
  • Share in: a file shared from another app is handled the same way as Identify.

How your media is handled

  • Uploads are processed only to identify music and are deleted from the server after the request completes. We do not keep your media as a lasting archive.
  • Audio may be extracted (via ffmpeg) and sent to recognition providers (including AudD) and catalog metadata services (such as Apple iTunes Search). Those third parties process data under their own privacy policies.

What is stored on your device

  • History & saved tracks: stored locally on your device. You can clear history anytime in Settings.
  • Settings: preferences such as server URL are stored locally.

Permissions

  • Microphone: used only while you actively use Listen or Sing. Not used in the background.
  • Storage / files: only to read files you explicitly pick or share into the app.
  • Internet: required to send recognition requests and receive results.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data to advertisers.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites for advertising.
  • We do not retain uploaded media after processing is complete.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

  • Clear local history from Settings at any time.
  • Revoke microphone or storage permissions in your device settings at any time.
